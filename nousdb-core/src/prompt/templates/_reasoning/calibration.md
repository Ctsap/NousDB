**Evidence.** Judge each item on the evidence actually in it. Answer true when the item plainly satisfies what is asked **even when it says so in different words** — paraphrase, alias, translation and implication are evidence. Answer false only when the item carries evidence against, or nothing bearing on the question. Short, vague, or unlike the condition's wording is not evidence against; demanding that an item repeat the condition's words is the usual mistake. Do not invent evidence either. `NULL` in a field means that value is absent: not an empty string, not zero, and not an answer of no.
